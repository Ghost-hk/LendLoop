# Surface brief: LendLoop app + public wall

Scope: `/app/*` (Operate) and `/` (Persuade, same world). Full spec in docs/sections/04-ui.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The neighborhood's shared tool wall, where a tool on loan leaves its painted outline behind. Refuses the neutral marketplace card grid with grey pills that every listing board ships.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F4F1EA ground, ink #1B1B1B rail, hardboard #C9A270 pegboard only behind tool walls, safety yellow #F4C20D as fill with ink text for active/primary, tag red #D7262E for overdue and counts. Barlow body at one size, Barlow Condensed uppercase for titles and tool names. Kraft hang tags carry status words.

STORY: A neighbor sees at a glance what is home and what is out, asks for dates in one dialog, and always knows where each loan stands and what happens next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink left rail (yellow wordmark, nav, red count on Incoming); paper content with condensed uppercase title, count line, "Hang a tool" safety-yellow primary button top right; category chips; pegboard wall of tiles, four across at desktop. Each tile: board panel with large category icon (solid ink stroke when home, dashed yellow stroke on ink when out), condensed name, category and condition, owner + Trusted mark, hang tag, action.

FORM: Shadow Board, my ranked candidate 1 of 7 (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user over the assigned direction). seed:ea7db85b470958a4
